# Quantum Physics — Lecture Slides

This folder contains the lecture slides developed for the **Quantum Physics** course at the **University of Valladolid (Spain)** as part of the **QPhys-3D** teaching innovation project.

The material follows the progression from the experimental origins of quantum physics to the mathematical formalism of quantum mechanics and its application to atomic systems, angular momentum, identical particles and many-electron atoms.

## Part I — Origins of Quantum Physics

| Chapter | Topic | Main contents |
|---|---|---|
| [Chapter 1](./Chapter_01.pdf) | **Thermal radiation and Planck's postulate** | Thermal radiation, blackbody radiation, classical cavity radiation and Planck's quantization hypothesis. |
| [Chapter 2](./Chapter_02.pdf) | **Photons: particle-like properties of radiation** | Photoelectric effect, photons, Compton scattering, X-ray production, pair production and annihilation. |
| [Chapter 3](./Chapter_03.pdf) | **de Broglie's postulate: wave-like properties of particles** | Wave-particle duality, two-slit interference, de Broglie waves, electron diffraction and the uncertainty principle. |
| [Chapter 4](./Chapter_04.pdf) | **Models of the Atom** | Historical atomic models, Thomson and Rutherford models, alpha-particle scattering and atomic spectra. |
| [Chapter 5](./Chapter_05.pdf) | **Bohr's Model of the Atom** | Bohr's postulates, quantized energy levels, atomic spectra, Franck-Hertz experiment and limitations of the old quantum theory. |

## Part II — Schrödinger Quantum Mechanics

| Chapter | Topic | Main contents |
|---|---|---|
| [Chapter 6](./Chapter_06.pdf) | **Schrödinger's Theory of Quantum Physics** | Schrödinger equation, wave functions, Born interpretation, expectation values, uncertainties and stationary states. |
| [Chapter 7](./Chapter_07.pdf) | **Solutions of the time-independent Schrödinger equation** | Free particle, infinite and finite wells, step and barrier potentials, tunnelling and the harmonic oscillator. |
| [Chapter 8](./Chapter_08.pdf) | **One-Electron Atoms** | Three-dimensional Schrödinger equation, central potentials, two-body problem and the hydrogen atom. |

## Part III — Mathematical Tools of Quantum Mechanics

This block introduces the mathematical formalism used throughout the rest of the course, following the structure of Chapter II of Cohen-Tannoudji.

| Chapter | Topic |
|---|---|
| [Chapter 9](./Chapter_09.pdf) | **One-particle wave function space** |
| [Chapter 10](./Chapter_10.pdf) | **State space and Dirac notation** |
| [Chapter 11](./Chapter_11.pdf) | **Representations in the state space** |
| [Chapter 12](./Chapter_12.pdf) | **Eigenvalue equations and observables** |
| [Chapter 13](./Chapter_13.pdf) | **Two important examples of representations and observables** |
| [Chapter 14](./Chapter_14.pdf) | **Tensor products and applications** |

The block develops state vectors, bra-ket notation, orthonormal bases, representations, operators and observables, eigenvalue problems, position and momentum representations, and tensor-product spaces.

## Part IV — Postulates of Quantum Mechanics

This block introduces the general postulates of quantum mechanics and their physical interpretation.

| Chapter | Topic |
|---|---|
| [Chapter 15](./Chapter_15.pdf) | **Statement of the postulates** |
| [Chapter 16](./Chapter_16.pdf) | **Physical interpretation of the postulates** |
| [Chapter 17](./Chapter_17.pdf) | **Physical implications of the Schrödinger equation** |

Topics include quantum states, observables, measurement probabilities, time evolution, superposition and the physical consequences of the Schrödinger equation.

## Part V — Angular Momentum and Spin

| Chapter | Topic | Main contents |
|---|---|---|
| [Chapter 18](./Chapter_18.pdf) | **Orbital angular momentum** | Angular-momentum operators, commutation relations, eigenvalues and eigenfunctions, magnetic moment and the Stern-Gerlach experiment. |
| [Chapter 19](./Chapter_19.pdf) | **General angular momentum** | General angular-momentum algebra, eigenvalues of \(J^2\) and \(J_z\), standard basis and angular-momentum state spaces. |
| [Chapter 20](./Chapter_20.pdf) | **Spin 1/2** | Spin-1/2 basis, matrix representation, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, spin along arbitrary directions and Pauli theory. |

| [Chapter 22](./Chapter_22.pdf) | **Addition of angular momenta: general method** | Addition rules for angular momenta, allowed \(J\) and \(M\) values, coupled and uncoupled bases, and Clebsch-Gordan coefficients. |

| Chapter | Topic | Main contents |

## Course context

These materials were developed for undergraduate teaching in **Quantum Physics** in the Physics degree at the **University of Valladolid**.

They are intended to accompany classroom explanations and include conceptual questions, derivations, examples and applications that progressively connect experimental evidence with the formal structure of quantum mechanics.

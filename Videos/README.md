# QPhys-3D Videos

This folder contains audiovisual teaching resources developed within the **QPhys-3D** teaching innovation project for the *Quantum Physics* course at the **University of Valladolid (Spain)**.

The videos are designed as short conceptual summaries that complement the lecture slides and other course materials.

## Video 1 — From Quanta to Quantum Mechanics

This video provides a visual overview of the main experimental and conceptual developments that led from classical physics to the emergence of quantum mechanics.

It combines narration, diagrams and **Manim animations** to illustrate key ideas such as:

- blackbody radiation and Planck's quantization hypothesis,
- the photoelectric effect,
- photons and Compton scattering,
- wave-particle duality,
- the double-slit experiment,
- de Broglie's matter waves,
- the uncertainty principle,
- Rutherford's nuclear model,
- atomic spectra,
- Bohr's model,
- the Franck-Hertz experiment,
- and the transition toward the Schrödinger description of quantum systems.

The video is approximately **8 minutes long** and is intended as a compact review of the conceptual foundations of quantum physics.

## Use in teaching

The video can be used:

- as a brief introduction to the course,
- as a review resource after the first block of the subject,
- as preparation before moving to the formal development of quantum mechanics,
- or as an autonomous study resource for students.
